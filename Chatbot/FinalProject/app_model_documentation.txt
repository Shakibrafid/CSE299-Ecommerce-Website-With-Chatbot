core: Manages universal, cross-cutting configurations and platform utility features.
Setting: Acts as a dynamic database knob to adjust global store parameters instantly without code redeploys.
Notification: Stores and routes transaction and chat updates as direct messaging feeds for specific users.

store: Handles the public-facing product catalog details, hierarchy, and physical stock accounting logs.
Category: Organizes products into searchable, multi-layered parent and sub-level structural groups.
Product: Represents individual saleable catalog entries with strict commercial pricing and standard core details.
ProductImage: Resolves multi-valued item gallery paths by storing supplementary, high-resolution product photos.
Inventory: Logs a tamper-proof historical transaction timeline whenever stock numbers are manually modified.

cart: Tracks temporary customer selections before they proceed to checkout.
Cart: Creates a persistent session or user-linked bucket to hold prospective purchases.
CartItem: Links specific catalog items and quantities together while enforcing a non-duplicate rule per shopping cart.

orders: Registers binding purchase records, revenue ledgers, legal receipts, and itemized billing lines.
Order: Captures overall transactional summaries, shipping locations, dynamic sub-totals, and operational fulfillment tracking milestones.
OrderItem: Freezes an absolute historical price and quantity snapshot of individual items at the exact checkout millisecond.
Payment: Maps transaction gateway response tokens and processing status updates directly to an order record.
Invoice: Tracks the legal reference numbers and generated file system pathways for customer billing PDFs.

chat: Runs the automated conversational infrastructure and emergency human intervention logs.
ChatSession: Establishes unique continuous tracking sessions that group streaming messages to individual user accounts.
ChatMessage: Archives individual text bubbles, marking the read/unread states and distinct roles of the senders.
ChatMessageAttachment: Splits out multi-valued media screenshots or document file uploads sent within text streams.
HumanSupportSession: Hosts a dedicated weak entity block detailing time windows when a live assistant overrides the automation.
